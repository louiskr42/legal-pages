Support

Help with the Sealed app

Get in touch

Something not working, a question about an event, or anything else about Sealed — email us and we'll get back to you:

ce.coreelements@gmail.com

Report a photo, voice memo, or text note

Use the Report button directly on the content inside the app — that reaches both the event's host and us, and we aim to act within 24 hours. You can also block another participant from the same screen.

Delete your account

Open Settings inside the app and choose Delete account. This is permanent and signs you out immediately — see our Privacy Policy for exactly what happens to your data and your events.

Common questions

Why can't I see any photos yet? Nothing is visible to anyone — including the person who took it and the event's host — until the host's chosen reveal time. That's by design, not a bug.

Can I join without installing the app? Not yet — everyone, hosts and guests, joins through the Sealed app itself.
