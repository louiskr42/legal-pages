4. How the Service works

  • A host creates an event with a start time, end time, and a reveal time. Guests join using an invite code, QR code, or link.
  • During the recording window, participants can take photos, record short voice memos (up to 30 seconds), and leave short text notes (up to 1,000 characters).
  • Nobody — including the person who captured it and the host — can view this content before the event's reveal time. This time-lock is enforced on our servers, based on server time, not on any setting in the app or on your device's clock. Section 10 explains the one necessary exception to this.
  • Once the reveal time passes, every participant can see everything captured during the event in a shared gallery, and download photos or play back voice memos.
  • A host can push the reveal time later, but never earlier, and only before the reveal has happened.
  • Captured content and the event itself are automatically and permanently deleted a set number of days after the reveal (currently 7 days) — see Section 8.
