5. The reveal time-lock, and an honest note about our own access

Whether an event is "revealed" is decided entirely by our servers comparing the current server time to the event's reveal time — never by a flag your device sends, and never by your device's clock. Until that moment, no participant can read any photo, voice memo, or text note from that event, including the person who created it and the event's host.

This protects participants from each other and from the host — it is not, and cannot be, a technical barrier against us as the operator. We hold administrative credentials to our database and file storage that can technically reach stored content, including before a reveal. We only use that access where necessary — for example to maintain the Service, fix a fault, investigate a report of abuse or illegal content, or comply with a legal obligation — and not to browse or view content as a matter of routine. No person manually previews photos, voice memos, or text notes before a reveal in the ordinary course of business.

7. Anonymous posting and account deletion

Choosing to post a voice memo or text note "Anonymous" only hides your display name from other guests. We and the event's host always retain the actual link between that content and your account, because we need it to act on reports and enforce our Terms.

If you delete your account, events you host — and all photos, voice memos, and text notes in them — are permanently deleted. Content you contributed to other people's events is not deleted, so the rest of that event's gallery stays intact for its other participants, but it is disassociated from your name, the same way anonymous content is shown. Your account itself is permanently disabled; it cannot sign in again, and your email address is removed from it.

11. Children

Sealed is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

12. Security

Content is stored in a private file storage bucket that nobody can read directly; the app only ever receives short-lived, signed links to individual files, and only after an event's reveal. Access to event data is enforced in our database through row-level security tied to server time, not to anything the app itself decides. Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
