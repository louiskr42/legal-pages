Restore purchases

Bought "Remove Ads" or "Sippit Premium" and reinstalled the app or switched devices? Open the help screen (the "?" button at the top of any game) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested there: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or your order history on Google Play.

Change your ad privacy choices

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, open the help screen and tap Change privacy settings to review or withdraw your consent for ads at any time. On iPhone you can also change tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Sippit works without sign-up, and player names stay on your device.

Does Sippit need an internet connection? The games work offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.
