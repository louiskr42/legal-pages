2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Sippit party game app on iOS and Android, why, who it is shared with, and what rights you have. It replaces all earlier versions of the Sippit privacy policy.

In short: Sippit has no accounts and no analytics. Player names and game settings stay on your device. If the app shows ads, Google AdMob processes device data for that — in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only after you consent. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat keeping track of what you've unlocked.

3. Data stored only on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us or any third party:

  • player names you enter for a game round;
  • your game settings (for example language, selected game modes, number of rounds);
  • a local record of which purchases are unlocked and when an ad was last shown, so the app works offline and respects ad limits.

You can delete this data at any time by removing players in the app or by uninstalling Sippit.

4. Data processed by third parties

4.1 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Sippit shows full-screen ads at the start or end of some game rounds, served by Google AdMob. For this, Google processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ads shown and your interaction with them (for example taps);
  • your consent choices.

Consent: If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, Sippit asks for your consent through Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. Depending on your choice, Google shows personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or limited ads. On iOS, Sippit additionally asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change your choices at any time (see Section 7).

Google acts as an independent controller for this processing. Details: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you have bought "Remove Ads" or "Sippit Premium", the ad SDK is not started, no ads are requested, and you will not see the consent or tracking dialogs.

4.2 In-app purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details, name, or email address.

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to verify purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat processes, on our behalf:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app;
  • purchase information (product, price, currency, store transaction ID, date);
  • technical data (device type, operating system, app version, country, IP address).

Details: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4.3 App stores

When you download Sippit, Apple or Google process data about your download under their own privacy policies. They may also provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example number of installs or crash counts by device) that do not identify you.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy (access);
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data (rectification);
  • ask us to delete your data (erasure);
  • ask us to restrict processing, or object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability); and
  • withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future.

Because Sippit has no accounts, we usually cannot link data to you. For purchase data, tell us your store transaction ID (from your Apple or Google receipt) so we can find it. Contact: ce.coreelements@gmail.com.

Sippit is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly process personal data of children under 16. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help remove any related data.
